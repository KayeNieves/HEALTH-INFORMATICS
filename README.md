# Health Informatics System

## About the Project

A digital space for personal health information, designed to bring medical records, health history, and appointment scheduling into one place.

The system aims to make healthcare information more accessible, organized, and easier to manage while giving users a more personal way to keep track of their health.

## Planned Features

- **Personal Health Records** – Store and organize personal medical information.
- **Medical History** – Keep track of previous illnesses, conditions, and treatments.
- **Appointment Booking** – Schedule healthcare consultations and appointments.
- **Health Profile** – Maintain a centralized profile containing important health details.
- **Health Overview** – View relevant health information in one place.

## Concept

The system serves as a personal archive of one's health journey.
